Request Account & Data Deletion

If you have created an account in the Route 254 app and wish to delete your account and associated personal data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to our support team with the following details:

  • Email To: realtrippyhq@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Route 254
  • Body: Please include the phone number or email address registered with your account.

Data Retention & Deletion Timeline

Upon receiving your request, we will verify your identity and permanently delete your user account details, auth profiles, and associated saved preferences within 30 days. Non-identifiable diagnostic logs may be retained for security purposes.
